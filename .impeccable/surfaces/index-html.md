# Surface brief: portfolio home and case study (index.html)

Scope: the whole first version of the site: landing, project index, one case-study template, about and contact. Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience and job: recruiters skimming, hiring managers reading closely, freelance clients. They must learn who Maia Branson is, see the work, and move from a project into its case study without a hard page jump. Content is placeholder until Maia supplies real projects; nothing may be invented as fact.

Constraints from the owner: motion must never be slow or showy; the site must read well on phones.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole site lives on one character grid. Living ASCII butterflies open it, then the same grid goes quiet under a clean sheet of work. It refuses the usual dark portfolio with a giant name, cursor follower and marquee.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy field with a faint cell grid and butterflies drawn as blue character cells; a cool paper sheet with rounded corners that slides over it; ink-blue text, one butterfly-blue accent; Host Grotesk for words, Geist Mono only for grid-native labels and the ASCII itself.

STORY: The visitor meets Maia by name, sees she can make motion feel effortless, scrolls into the work, opens a project and reads it, then finds how to get in touch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height navy butterfly field. Name at display size, lower left, decoding into place in under half a second; one or two intro sentences beneath it; small navigation top right; a "See the work" link at the bottom edge. Butterflies flock, steer around the text and scatter from the pointer or a tap.

FORM: Pinned by the owner, not rolled. seed: none (pinned) "the butterflies from this as the landing page with my name and introduction", "Dark landing, light work", "Yes, build it" to: ASCII butterfly landing on a character grid, clean project cards, cards that grow into the case study, a brief decode effect on the name. Signature interaction: a project cover grows into the case-study header (same-document view transition, about 450ms). Motion grammar: quick exponential ease-outs, grid-stepped canvas motion, one scroll-linked focus pull on the disciplines list.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
