# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework and no build step, so it can be hosted anywhere. Deploy target: undecided.

## Users

The site is for a junior designer looking for their first roles or clients. Three groups will read it, and none of them is primary:

- **Recruiters** screening quickly, often in under a minute, to decide whether to pass the candidate on.
- **Hiring managers and designers** reading case studies closely to judge thinking, process and craft.
- **Freelance clients** (founders, small businesses) deciding whether to hire for a project.

All three need to grasp who Fili is, what they do and how to reach them within the first screen. Readers who stay need enough depth to judge real work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that wins interviews and freelance work for a junior designer. The repository is also a place to try out several design directions for the same portfolio before settling on one ("testing different designs"). Success means a reader comes away with a clear sense of the person and the work, and gets in touch.

## Positioning

Undecided. The owner has not yet named what sets them apart from other juniors. Future work must not invent a tagline or claim, such as "design engineer" or "full-stack designer", until the owner confirms one.

## Operating Context

- People open the site from job applications, résumés, LinkedIn or direct outreach, often on a phone and between other tasks.
- Several design directions may live side by side in the repo for comparison. Each must present the same product truth (content, contact, projects).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Target discipline: mixed or undecided. The owner's work spans more than one design area, so the structure must not lock the site into a single discipline (for example UX-only case studies).
- Single language: English.
- Static site; nothing server-side is required.
- Expected content: a home or intro, a project index, per-project case studies, an about section and contact. Exact information architecture: undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Fili" (from the repository name; full display name and how they want to be credited still to be confirmed).
- No voice, logo or identity assets exist yet.

## Evidence on Hand

- The owner has shipped or client work, school or course projects and personal or concept projects. **None are written up yet**: no case-study text, images or project names are in the repo.
- Until real content arrives, projects appear as clearly labelled placeholders. Never fabricate project names, clients, results, metrics, testimonials, employers or awards.
- No CV, photo, social links or contact address are in the repo yet. Ask for them; don't invent them.

## Product Principles

1. **Real work over claims.** The projects make the case; copy supports them and never inflates.
2. **Legible in the first screen.** Who, what and how to reach them should be clear to a recruiter in seconds.
3. **Depth on demand.** Close readers can go deeper into process and decisions without that depth blocking skimmers.
4. **Open to range.** The structure accommodates work from different disciplines until the owner picks a focus.
5. **Comparable directions.** Design explorations share one content source of truth so they can be compared fairly.
